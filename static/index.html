Step 1 · Define the Business Objective

Understand the problem before touching any data.

Data science process

Churn vs stay in our data

Problem

A telecom company is losing customers. It wants to know who is about to leave so it can offer retention deals before they go.

Objective

Build a model that predicts whether a customer will churn (leave) or stay, based on account details.

Problem type

Binary classification: Churn = 1 Stay = 0

Success measure

High recall and F1-score on churners, not just accuracy, because missing a churner costs more than a false alarm.
